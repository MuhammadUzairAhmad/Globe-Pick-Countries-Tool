Natural Earth populated places: public-domain data.

Authors: Natural Earth contributors, including Tom Patterson and Nathaniel Vaughn Kelso.
Terms and public-domain declaration:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Dataset description:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-populated-places/

GlobePick adapts a pinned Natural Earth source for country-scoped city/town selection.
Names, administrative labels, and approximate points are a dated cartographic snapshot.
This is selected coverage, not a complete register of cities or a current boundary authority.
No population values or source capital classifications are displayed as current facts.
The source commit, adaptation method, exclusion counts, per-country coverage, and checksum
are available in /data/cities/manifest.json. Adapted city records remain public domain.
