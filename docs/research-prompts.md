# Country project question review

The Phase 4 question sets are authored templates in `src/features/learning/country-project.ts`, reviewed during implementation on 14 October 2026. They are not generated by a model at runtime, and do not add unverified country facts to the dataset. This is an editorial review, not curriculum accreditation or an independent educator assessment.

All three topics have four questions. Each question asks for evidence and has a corresponding teacher-guidance paragraph. Learners record sources; open questions are assessed by their explanation rather than a fabricated answer key. Teachers should adapt the activity to their learners and can use additional sheets for longer answers and sketches.

Review checks:

- Location questions allow nearby countries or islands and do not assume every country has a land border. Continent conventions may differ between sources.
- Capital questions explicitly allow multiple cities and government seats. Teacher reference material preserves South Africa’s three roles, Nauru’s lack of an official capital, and territorial-status/transition notes from the existing reviewed dataset.
- Language questions ask about a specific example, without claiming that a country has a single language or that the dataset lists every language used.
- Currency questions ask for the date of a source and allow multiple currencies and changes over time. Reference facts remain a dated snapshot.
- Landscape questions let the learner choose a feature that exists; mountains, coastlines, and rivers are examples rather than required features.
- Route questions concern map reading. Teacher guidance explains why a map alone does not establish transport access, permissions, or real travel conditions.
- Community questions ask about a named place and sourced example, without treating one speaker, tradition, or food as representative of everyone. No contact with strangers or personal information is required.
- Source-comparison questions ask who wrote a source and when, what evidence it contains, and what remains uncertain.

Teacher facts are adapted from the same `Country` records used by the existing tools. Each teacher page includes the dataset review date, distinct source URLs, capital/country notes, attribution, and the database license link. The original provenance and review limitations remain in `src/data/countries/manifest.json` and the About page.

Assignments are built once after a user action. Preview and print receive the same assignment objects and question text. Paper-size and teacher-note controls do not rerun selection. Student worksheets appear first; teacher pages are appended only when requested.
